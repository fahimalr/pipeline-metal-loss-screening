Metal-Loss Screening Tool

Checks external corrosion features against ASME B31G, Modified B31G (0.85dL) and DNV-RP-F101, works out the estimated repair factor (ERF) and remaining life at a chosen growth rate, and ranks an in-line inspection anomaly list into a dig list.

Methods

ASME B31G (original)

Flow stress 1.1·SMYS, parabolic area 2/3·d·L. With z = L²/(D·t) and M = √(1+0.8z), for z ≤ 20: S = Sflow·(1 − 2/3·d/t)/(1 − 2/3·d/t/M). For z > 20: S = Sflow·(1 − d/t). Failure pressure P = 2·S·t/D.

Modified B31G (0.85dL)

Flow stress SMYS + 69 MPa, area 0.85·d·L. M = √(1 + 0.6275z − 0.003375z²) for z ≤ 50, else M = 0.032z + 3.3. S = Sflow·(1 − 0.85·d/t)/(1 − 0.85·d/t/M), P = 2·S·t/D.

DNV-RP-F101 (Part B)

Capacity of a single defect under internal pressure, based on tensile strength: Q = √(1 + 0.31·(L/√(D·t))²), P = 2·t·SMTS·(1 − d/t)/((D − t)·(1 − (d/t)/Q)). Shown without partial safety factors.

Safe pressure is failure pressure times the design factor F, and ERF = MAOP / safe pressure. Remaining life assumes depth grows linearly at the set rate with length held constant, and ends when ERF reaches 1 or depth reaches 80% of wall. Priority: Immediate if the worst ERF ≥ 1 or depth ≥ 80%; Scheduled if ERF ≥ 0.9 or remaining life is under 5 years; otherwise Monitor. The governing result is the most conservative of the three methods.

This is a portfolio and screening demonstration, not a substitute for an engineering assessment under CSA Z662 and the operator's integrity management program. It applies to blunt metal loss only, not cracks, dents or features in welds. The sample list is synthetic data generated for the demo.
